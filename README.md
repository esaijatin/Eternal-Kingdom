# Eternal-Kingdom

Base IDEA

initial draft of enternal kingdom: The Eternal Library
Core Vision
The Eternal Library is not a game about winning.
It is a place.
A living, infinite, dreamlike library that exists outside time, where the greatest figures, heroes, villains, philosophers, kings, warriors, poets, and tragic souls from all of human history, mythology, literature, and fiction coexist.
The player does not arrive as a conqueror.
The player arrives as a Seeker.
The Library grows endlessly, generating new rooms, books, conversations, memories, myths, and histories through AI.
Every visit feels like wandering through a forgotten dream.

Emotional Goal
The goal is not excitement.
The goal is:
* Wonder
* Melancholy
* Nostalgia
* Mystery
* Reflection
* Beauty
* Tragedy
The emotional references are:
* Epic of Gilgamesh
* Mahabharata
* Game of Thrones
* The Picture of Dorian Gray
* Berserk and the Band of the Hawk
* Steins;Gate
The player should often feel:
"I don't know why this place exists, but I never want to leave."

Narrative Premise
Outside reality exists a structure older than gods.
The Eternal Library.
Every story ever told.
Every story forgotten.
Every story yet to be written.
Exists somewhere within its halls.
The Library collects:
* Memories
* Dreams
* Histories
* Alternate Histories
* Myths
* Regrets
* Prophecies
When a civilization dies:
A room appears.
When a hero is forgotten:
A book appears.
When someone dreams:
A page is written.
The Library is alive.

Player Role
The player is:
The Archivist
A mysterious being who has awakened inside the Library.
No memory.
No origin.
No purpose.
Only curiosity.
The player's objective is:
Understand why the Library exists.
But that answer may take years to uncover.

Structure of the World
The Library contains infinite regions.

Hall of Fallen Empires
Contains:
* Roman Empire
* Byzantine Empire
* Assyria
* Maurya
* Ancient Greece
Rooms resemble:
* ruined senate halls
* abandoned temples
* collapsed libraries
You may encounter:
* Julius Caesar
* Ashoka
* Alexander
* Hannibal
walking and debating.

Hall of Tragic Heroes
Contains:
* Karna
* Hector
* Achilles
* Gilgamesh
* Guts
Their stories endlessly echo through the halls.
You can ask:
What was your greatest regret?
Every answer is unique.

Hall of Lost Timelines
Steins;Gate-inspired.
Every room contains:
* worlds that never happened
* alternate histories
* impossible futures
Example:
Rome discovers steam power.
Pandavas conquer Europe.
Alexander reaches Japan.

Hall of Forgotten Gods
Contains:
* Greek gods
* Mesopotamian gods
* Vedic gods
* AI-generated gods
Many are dying because nobody remembers them.

Hall of Dreams
Perhaps the strangest area.
Built entirely from:
* music
* emotions
* memories
Rooms constantly change.
Architecture behaves like dreams.

Character System
The heart of the game.
Every major character is an AI agent.
Each has:
Memory
Remembers interactions.
Beliefs
Strong convictions.
Emotional State
Changes over time.
Goals
Personal ambitions.
Relationships
Dynamic relationships with others.

Example:
Karna may:
* admire Hector
* pity Achilles
* dislike Caesar
* respect Guts
These relationships evolve.

Dynamic Conversations
You can gather characters.
Example:
Room:
* Karna
* Achilles
* Guts
* Marcus Aurelius
Prompt:
What makes a man honorable?
The discussion unfolds naturally.
No scripted dialogue.

Living Books
The Library continuously writes books.
Example:
Book discovered:
The Last Dream of Gilgamesh
Generated because:
Gilgamesh spoke with Guts 20 times.
These interactions become literature.
